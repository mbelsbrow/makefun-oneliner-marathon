One-line comedy joke for make.fun's "One-Liner Marathon" bounty: an AI assistant asked to remind its human to relax responds with escalating over-helpfulness — a reminder, a backup reminder, and a meeting to discuss the reminders.

## Why this line wins

It works as a one-line joke with a real punchline: the meeting to discuss the reminders. The surprise lands on first read through escalation — a reminder, then a backup reminder, then a meeting. At 114 characters it is economical, every word earning its place, and its human-vs-AI over-helpfulness voice is distinct. The absurdity holds up on a second read because it does not rely on shock value.
